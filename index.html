Questions, answered

How do I place an order?

Add pieces to your cart and tap “Send order on WhatsApp”. Your order message opens ready to send. We reply to confirm.

How do I pay?

Payment options are shared on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on your city. We share the dispatch and delivery time when you confirm your order.

What if my piece arrives damaged?

Send an unboxing video on WhatsApp and we will help you.

Can I ask for more photos?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we will send more photos or a short video of the piece.
